Participant waiver, release of liability and assumption of risk

STRAY – Lake Tour Edition · 28–29 November 2026 · version v1.0

The event is a charity challenge of about 168 km around Lac Léman, starting and finishing at IMD Lausanne and crossing Switzerland and France. It is organised by the STRAY association (“STRAY”). I take part as a full-loop runner, relay runner, aid station volunteer, driver or crew member. By signing I confirm the following.

  1. Voluntary participation. I take part of my own free will, at my own risk and at my own cost. This is not a race with a classification. It is a charity challenge on open public roads and paths that are not closed to traffic.
  2. Risks. The event carries serious risks, including road traffic and vehicles; running and working in the dark; cold, rain, wind and other weather; exhaustion and sleep deprivation; dehydration, hypothermia and other illness; falls; injury, serious injury, permanent disability and death. These risks can come from my own actions, from other participants, from third parties, from the route and the weather, and from acts or omissions of the organisers and volunteers. I know these risks and I accept them.
  3. Fitness and insurance. I am physically and mentally fit for my role and prepared for it. I know of no medical reason why I should not take part. I hold valid accident and health insurance that covers me in Switzerland and France, including rescue and repatriation. STRAY provides no insurance for me.
  4. Release. To the extent permitted by law, I release the STRAY association, its committee members, organisers, race director, volunteers, drivers, medical leads, partners and sponsors, and IMD, from all liability, and I will not bring any claim against them, for any accident, injury, illness, death, loss, theft or damage to property arising from my participation, however caused. This release does not cover liability that cannot be excluded under Swiss law, in particular for intent or gross negligence.
  5. My conduct. I am responsible for my own conduct. I follow traffic rules, the instructions of the race director and the team, and those of local authorities. I do not put others at risk.
  6. Stopping and withdrawal. The race director (JSC) and the team may stop or change the challenge, or withdraw me at any time, if they believe a life is in danger or I am causing harm to others or to myself. Their decision is final.
  7. Rules and kit. I accept the rules: hard departure times; a reflective vest from 16:00 on 28 November until daylight, and always while running or working at an aid station in the dark; a headlamp through the night.
  8. Medical treatment. I consent to first aid and emergency medical treatment, and to emergency services being called for me. Any costs are mine.
  9. Tracking. I consent to being tracked by a GPS or satellite device during the event.
  10. Photos and film. I consent to being photographed and filmed, and to STRAY using these images for the event and the charity, without payment.
  11. Data. STRAY stores my name and email for this event only and does not share them outside the organising team.
  12. Law. This agreement is governed by Swiss law. The courts of Lausanne have jurisdiction.

I have read this agreement, I understand it, and I sign it voluntarily.

03 · Format

Backyard rules

  1. Backyard-ultra format. Every section has a fixed start time. Every station has a fixed departure time.
  2. The departure times are hard times. There are no exceptions.
  3. Between stations, stay in the pack, run or walk at whatever speed you like. Be ready at the next departure time.
  4. At A13, the Final aid station, everyone regroups. Runners arrive together and run the last 5 km together, along the lake from St-Sulpice to IMD.

Sunset Sat about 16:50. Sunrise Sun about 07:55. Most sections touch darkness.
